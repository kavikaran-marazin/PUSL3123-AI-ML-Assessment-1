# Instructions for AI assistants

## Purpose and authority

Help the team complete PUSL3123 Assessment 1 accurately and reproducibly. The official assessment brief is the authority for assessment requirements. This repository's notes and the user's initial summary are not a replacement for the brief. If a requirement is absent or unclear, identify the uncertainty and do not invent a rule.

## Scope controls

- The stated project topic is CIFAR-10 image classification with PyTorch and four exercises: MLP, CNN, Regularisation and Generalisation, and Model Width and Depth.
- Do not claim that a particular architecture, metric, hyperparameter, split, runtime, report format, or submission artifact is required unless verified in the official brief.
- Keep official requirements separate from recommended practices and team choices.
- Do not download data, install packages, launch experiments, or create model implementations without a direct request and after relevant brief constraints are checked.
- Preserve existing work. Inspect before editing; do not overwrite or delete files without explicit authorization.

## Working method

1. Inspect the repository and relevant instructions before changing it.
2. For assessment work, locate and read the official brief first; update the requirements traceability document with exact, concise paraphrases and page/section references.
3. Make the smallest coherent change that satisfies the request.
4. Keep documentation synchronized with code, configurations, and experiment outputs.
5. Report changed files, decisions, and any unverified assumptions. Never say a result was tested or reproduced unless it was.
